How RestMalta works

RestMalta has a few moving parts — dossiers, e-signed leases, move-in inventories. This page walks through exactly what happens, step by step, for whichever role fits you.

👋 Looking for a flat? Everything below happens from your tenant dashboard once you sign up — no separate app, no downloads.
01
🔍

Browse & filter

Search by zone, budget, and lease type — short-let, long-let, or a room in a shared flat. Switch to map view to see everything geographically, or watch a virtual tour video if the lessor added one.

02
💬

Message directly, no numbers shared

Real-time chat with the lessor or agency, right in the app. Your phone number stays private until you choose to share it — no more handing it out to strangers on Facebook groups.

03
📋

Build your dossier once

Upload your ID, an employment letter, bank statements and references a single time. From then on, every application reuses the same verified dossier — no re-uploading for each flat you're interested in.

04
👥

Bringing roommates?

Add co-lessees directly to your application — they each get invited to submit their own dossier and sign the lease alongside you. Searching for people to share with in the first place? Find a Roommate connects you with others looking too.

05
🤝

Apply

Send your application with your move-in date and any notes for the lessor. If there's a platform fee for this listing, it's authorized on your card at this point — but only actually charged if the lessor accepts. Nothing is taken upfront.

06
✍️

Lease generated & e-signed

The moment you're accepted, a lease compliant with Maltese rental law is generated automatically with your details already filled in. Review and sign it from your phone — no printing, no in-person meeting required.

07
📸

Move-in inventory

Before you get the keys, every room and both meters (water & electricity) are photographed and logged, then signed by both you and your lessor. This becomes your proof of the property's condition — real protection if there's ever a dispute over the deposit.

08
🏡

Live & manage

Your dashboard tracks every payment due, lets you report maintenance issues with photos, and keeps your full conversation history with your lessor. Deposit and rent go directly to your lessor's account — RestMalta never holds your money.

09
📅

Renewing or leaving

When your lease is coming to an end, renew it or generate your official notice to leave — both handled right from the app, correctly timed to Maltese lease law. Leave a review of your lessor when you're done, and see the reviews they've left for you too.

A few things worth explaining

RestMalta does more than a typical listings site — here's the "why" behind the parts that aren't obvious at first glance.

📸 What's the "move-in inventory"?

Formally called an EDL (état des lieux) in Maltese rental practice — a room-by-room record of the property's condition and both meter readings, photographed and signed by both parties the day the tenant moves in. Its whole purpose is to settle disputes fairly later — if something's disputed at move-out, there's an agreed, dated record from move-in to compare against.

🪪 Why does RestMalta need my ID?

Two reasons. First, trust — a landlord reviewing your application, or a tenant reviewing who they're renting from, can see a verified identity instead of just a name in a chat. Second, Maltese leases legally require each party's identification to appear on the document itself, so it's needed either way.

💶 What's the platform fee actually for?

It covers running the verification, e-signing and hosting behind the platform. It's only ever charged if a lessee's application is actually accepted — never upfront, and never if you're rejected. Properties managed by an agency don't carry this fee at all, since agencies pay their own separate subscription instead.

🏢 Do I need an agency, or can I self-manage?

Self-managing directly between lessor and lessee is the default and works for most people — RestMalta handles the lease, e-signing and move-in inventory either way. An agency is there for landlords who'd rather hand off the day-to-day (visits, applications, coordination) entirely.

⚖️ Is the lease actually legally valid?

Leases generated through RestMalta are built to comply with Malta's Private Residential Leases Act (Chapter 604) — covering the property address, agreed use, duration, rent and payment method, deposit, and the move-in inventory as required by law. It remains the landlord's responsibility to register the lease with the Housing Authority within the legally required timeframe.

🔑 What happens to my deposit and rent?

They're never held by RestMalta. Once a lease is signed, deposit and first month's rent go straight from the tenant's payment method to the landlord's own account — the platform only ever facilitates the transaction, it isn't a party to it.
