How RestMalta works

A different experience depending on who you are — pick your role below to see exactly what happens, step by step.

01
🔍

Browse & message

Filter by zone, budget and type. Message lessors or agents directly through real-time chat — no phone numbers shared, no vanishing "agents" from a WhatsApp group. Searching alone? Find a Roommate connects you with others looking to share.

02
🤝

Apply & sign

A small platform fee is authorized on your card when you apply — never charged unless the lessor accepts. Once accepted, your lease is auto-generated and e-signed, compliant with Maltese law. Bringing roommates? They're added to the same lease and sign too.

03
📸

Move in with proof

Every room and both meters are logged and photographed at move-in, signed by you and your lessor. Real protection if a dispute ever comes up — not just a promise. Deposit and first month go straight to your lessor, no middleman holding your money.

04
🏡

Live & manage

Message your lessor anytime, report maintenance issues with photos, and track every payment from your dashboard. When you're ready, renew your lease or generate your official notice to leave — both handled right from the app.

01
✍️

List your property

Fill in the details yourself — title, photos, price, you're in control. Selling instead? List it for sale too — same platform, different flow.

02
💬

Review & accept

Lessees message and apply directly — see their verified dossier (ID, employment letter, references) before deciding. Accept the application that fits, and their lease generates automatically, ready to sign.

03
📋

Move-in day

Complete the move-in inventory with photos and meter readings, right from your dashboard — signed by both of you as shared proof. Deposit and first month land directly in your account, no waiting on a middleman.

04
📊

Manage with ease

Track every rented property, payment status and lease end date in one place. Handle maintenance requests, message your lessee, and renew or let leases run their course — all from Rented Properties.
