Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember your preferences, and — where used — understand how visitors use the site.

2. Cookies we use

TypePurposeConsent required?
EssentialKeeping you logged in, remembering your session, basic site functionalityNo — required for the site to work
PreferencesRemembering settings such as language or display choicesNo, or implied
AnalyticsUnderstanding how visitors use the site, to improve itYes, where used

RestMalta does not currently use third-party advertising cookies.

3. Third-party services

Some features rely on third-party providers that may set their own cookies or use similar technologies when their services load in your browser, including Clerk (authentication), Stripe (payments) and Leaflet/OpenStreetMap (maps). Each operates under its own privacy and cookie practices.

4. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of RestMalta — such as staying logged in — from working correctly.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the cookies we use change. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal data more broadly.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies can be sent via our Contact page.
